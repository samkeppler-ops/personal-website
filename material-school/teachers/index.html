Evidence for your district 📊

Asking your school or district to fund supplies? These are the numbers.

🖍️

Teacher-requested supplies raise test scores and keep teachers

About $400 in teacher-requested supplies leads to 2 to 9 more students scoring basic or above, and a 14% reduction in teacher turnover. We studied teacher projects funded on DonorsChoose in Pennsylvania public schools.

Test scores (Management Science, 2022) · Teacher turnover (M&SOM, 2025)

Where to get supplies 🎒

🍎

DonorsChoose

Post a classroom project and let donors fund it. The platform buys and ships the supplies to you.
🏫

AdoptAClassroom.org

Donors give to a teacher’s or school’s page, and teachers spend the funds on supplies from partner vendors.
✏️

Kids In Need Foundation

Free school supplies for teachers and students in high-poverty schools.
🧩

Lakeshore Learning

Hands-on classroom materials, manipulatives, and furniture, mostly for early childhood and elementary.
📚

Scholastic

Books and classroom libraries, including book clubs where classroom orders earn free books for the teacher.
