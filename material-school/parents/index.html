👪 For parents

Groups working on screens and kids

Parents across the country are organizing around phones in school, smartphones at home, and screens in the classroom. These groups share policy trackers, pledges, and ways to get involved.

Phones and smartphones 📵

🏫

Distraction-Free Schools

Tracks phone policy work in every state. See their phone-free schools section.
🧒

Smartphone Free Childhood US

A parent movement to delay smartphones. They run an active WhatsApp group you can ask to join.
⏳

Wait Until 8th

A pledge to wait on a smartphone until at least the end of 8th grade. It takes effect once ten families from your child’s grade and school sign.
📖

The Anxious Generation movement

Grew out of Jonathan Haidt’s book. Four norms: no smartphones before high school, no social media before 16, phone-free schools, and more independent play.
👩‍👧

Mothers Against Media Addiction (MAMA)

A parent-led group with local chapters, working on phone-free schools and online safety for kids.
💪

ScreenStrong

Guidance for families on delaying smartphones, social media, and video games at home.

Screens and AI in the classroom 💻

📂

Schools Beyond Screens EdTech Resource Library

A shared library of EdTech resources from Schools Beyond Screens and Distraction-Free Schools. Their EdTech and AI work group meets online the 2nd and 4th Wednesday of each month at noon Eastern.
🛡️

Tech-Safe Learning Coalition

Parents and educators asking schools to make sure the technology students use is safe and shown to help learning.
🌐

Children’s Screen Time Action Network

A network from Fairplay for parents, educators, and clinicians working on kids and screens, including EdTech in schools.
✅

Safe & Purposeful EdTech School Pledge

From ISTE+ASCD. Schools and districts sign it to commit to safe, purposeful technology use and family involvement. You can ask whether your school has signed.

Play and independence 🛝

🌱

Let Grow

Free programs for schools and families that give kids more unstructured play and independence.
🔬

Children and Screens

Research summaries and expert webinars on how digital media affects child development.

In Ann Arbor 🌳

🤝

ScreenWise Ann Arbor Parent Pact

Families agree to delay smartphones and social media together. You can see who has already signed.
