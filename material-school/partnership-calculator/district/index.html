What this doesn't tell you

This counts partnerships. It says nothing about what any of them are worth. A school with six well-matched partners may be doing better by its students than a school with twelve loosely connected ones, and this tool cannot see that.

It also assumes each school's pace holds steady, and that a long partnership takes about as much work to land as a short one. Those are spelled out on the reasoning page.
